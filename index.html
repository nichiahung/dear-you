To

Today, you turn —,
but to me, you are still the moment
my heart first learned how to love.
— days of loving you
my first thought in the morning,
my last breath at night,
and every quiet moment between.
我清晨的第一個念頭，
夜裡的最後一次呼吸，
以及之間所有靜謐的瞬間。
— with all my love —
Dearest

Marlene
